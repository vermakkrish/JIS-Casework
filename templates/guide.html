A LITTLE ORIENTATION

Workspace guide

Seven workflows. Three roles. One consistent record.

01 · REGISTRAR

Organize the work.

  1. Create Judge and Lawyer accounts in People & access. Share credentials securely.
  2. Register a case with parties, details, and assignments. Keep the generated CIN.
  3. Open a case to schedule a future weekday hearing. Available one-hour slots are shown in IST.
  4. Reschedule or adjourn with a reason. Adjournment releases the slot; rescheduling books a new one.
  5. Update assignments with a reason. Reassign a Judge’s open cases before removing their access.
02 · JUDGE

Complete the record.

  1. Find your assigned cases in the register or overview.
  2. After a hearing starts, record its summary. Saving marks that hearing completed.
  3. Once no booked hearings remain, record a verdict and judgment summary.
  4. Confirm the final action. The judgment is saved and the case closes together.

Summaries and judgments are final records. Closed cases cannot be reopened or edited.

03 · LAWYER

Stay informed.

  1. Search your represented cases by CIN, title, or party.
  2. Filter by status, type, and filing dates.
  3. Read case details, hearing histories, summaries, and judgments.
  4. Check the hearing agenda for your assigned cases.

Your role has read-only access. Ask the Registrar about assignment or scheduling changes.

Details that matter

Time: Asia/Kolkata (UTC+05:30). Slots run Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00, one hour each. This demonstration does not infer public holidays.

History: Deactivated accounts remain as historical authors. Removed case assignments stay in the case timeline.

Security: Change your initial password. Sign out on shared devices. Contact the project operator for password recovery; there is no email integration.

Demonstration: Use fictional information only. The system is a student project and does not represent an official court or provide legal advice.
